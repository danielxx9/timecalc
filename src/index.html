          Guide to time calculation
          
        
          What is a time duration calculator?
          
        
          A time duration calculator works out how much time has passed between two moments, or how much time will pass before a future moment. You enter a start time and an end time, and the calculator returns the elapsed duration in hours, minutes and seconds, plus common alternates such as decimal hours and total minutes.
          
        
          Duration calculations appear everywhere: timesheets for payroll, freelance invoices, project tracking, travel planning, meeting scheduling across time zones, and everyday questions like how long until a deadline. Doing the arithmetic by hand is error-prone once you cross midnight, subtract breaks, or work with more than two intervals.
          
        
          This tool handles those cases directly. It detects overnight periods automatically, splits durations into days and hours, and shows a visual timeline so you can see the interval at a glance.
          
        
          How to calculate time duration
          
        
          The basic calculation is straightforward: subtract the start time from the end time.
          
        
          1. Convert both times into a common unit, such as seconds since midnight.
          2. Subtract the start value from the end value.
          3. If the result is negative and the two times are on the same date, add 24 hours to handle an overnight shift.
          4. Convert the result back into hours, minutes and seconds.
          
        
          For example, from 09:00 to 17:30 the difference is 8.5 hours, or 8 hours 30 minutes. From 22:00 to 06:30 the raw subtraction gives a negative number, so 24 hours is added, producing 8 hours 30 minutes.
          
        
          Subtracting breaks
          
        
          When you need net working time, subtract each break from the gross duration. If a shift runs 09:00 to 17:30 and includes a 30-minute unpaid break, the net working time is 8 hours, not 8 hours 30 minutes. The Work Hours calculator handles this automatically, and the Break Calculator shows the effect of each break on its own.
          
        
          Adding intervals together
          
        
          When you have several work periods in a day, sum each one and check for overlaps. The Multiple Intervals calculator totals any number of intervals, flags overlaps and duplicates, and can merge adjacent periods or account for short idle gaps.
          
        
          Converting minutes to decimal hours
          
        
          Payroll systems and invoicing tools usually express time as decimal hours rather than hours and minutes. The conversion is simple: divide the minutes by 60.

          Overnight and multi-day shifts
          
        
          Overnight shifts are one of the most common sources of error in manual time arithmetic. If a shift starts at 22:00 and ends at 06:30, subtracting start from end gives −15.5 hours, which is meaningless. The correct approach treats the end time as belonging to the next day, so the duration becomes 8.5 hours.
          
        
          The Time Duration calculator automatically detects this case when both times are on the same date. If you have explicitly entered a later end date, the calculator uses the actual calendar difference instead.
          
        
          Business days and working days
          
        
          Business days exclude weekends and optionally a set of holiday dates. This is useful for project planning, contract deadlines, and loan or leave calculations where a stated number of working days matters.
          
        
          The Business Days calculator supports two modes: counting working days between two dates, or adding/subtracting business days from a starting date. Holiday dates must be entered in ISO format (YYYY-MM-DD).
          
        
          Time zones and daylight saving
          
        
          Time zone conversion is more subtle than it looks. Zones shift by one hour during daylight saving transitions, and different countries switch on different dates.
          
        
          The Time Zone Converter uses IANA time zone identifiers such as America/New_York or Europe/Paris and the browser's built-in Intl APIs. This makes it aware of historical and current daylight saving rules without requiring any external service or API key.
          
        
          Timesheet rounding
          
        
          Many employers round clock-in and clock-out times to the nearest 1, 5, 6, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes before calculating hours. Some round up; some round down; some round only the total duration.
          
        
          The Rounding calculator compares an exact duration against all common rounding rules at once, showing the difference each rule introduces.
          
        
          Worked examples
          
        
          Example 1 — Standard work shift
          
        
          • Gross shift: 17:30 − 09:00 = 8 hours 30 minutes
          • Break: 30 minutes unpaid
          • Net working time: 8 hours 30 minutes − 30 minutes = 8 hours
          • Decimal hours: 8.00
          
        
          Example 2 — Overnight shift
          
        
          • Start: 22:00 (22.0 hours since midnight)
          • End: 06:30 (6.5 hours since midnight)
          • Raw difference: 6.5 − 22.0 = −15.5 hours
          • Add 24 hours for the overnight period: −15.5 + 24 = 8.5 hours
          • Result: 8 hours 30 minutes
          
        
          Example 3 — Multiple intervals
          
        
          • 09:00–11:30 = 2h 30m
          • 12:30–15:00 = 2h 30m
          • 16:00–18:15 = 2h 15m
          • Total: 7 hours 15 minutes
          
        
          Example 4 — Time zone meeting
          
        
          • London (Europe/London): 14:00 GMT
          • New York (America/New_York): 09:00 EST
          • Dubai (Asia/Dubai): 18:00 GST
          • Tokyo (Asia/Tokyo): 23:00 JST

          How many working hours are in a month?
          Payroll · 4 min read
          
          
          
            
          The number of working hours in a month depends on how many weekdays the month contains, which varies between 20 and 23. A standard 8-hour working day gives 160 to 184 hours per month, but most payroll systems calculate against a fixed annual average.

          How to calculate timesheet hours in Excel and Google Sheets
          Tutorial · 5 min read
          
          
          
            
          Basic setup
          
            
          1. Put clock-in times in column A and clock-out times in column B.
          2. Format both columns as Time.
          3. In column C, enter =B2-A2.
          
            
          Handling overnight shifts
          
            
          Use =MOD(B2-A2, 1) to correctly handle shifts that cross midnight.
          
            
          Converting to decimal hours
          
            
          Multiply the time result by 24: =(B2-A2)*24.
          
            
          Totalling a week
          
            
          Change format to [h]:mm to display total hours beyond 24.

          Timesheet rounding rules by country
          Payroll · 4 min read
          
          
          
            
          United States
          
            
          The Fair Labor Standards Act permits rounding as long as it does not consistently favour the employer. Common intervals are 5, 6, or 15 minutes.
          
            
          United Kingdom
          
            
          There is no legal rounding standard. Any rounding must not reduce pay below the National Minimum Wage for hours actually worked.
          
            
          Germany
          
            
          Rounding is permitted only if it is reasonable for the employee. The Federal Labour Court has ruled that a 5-minute rounding rule is generally acceptable.
          
            
          Australia
          
            
          Fair Work regulations allow rounding to the nearest 6 minutes or, in some industries, 15 minutes.
          
            
          India
          
            
          Rounding is not standardised. Many employers use the grace time system.

          Frequently asked questions
          
      
          How do I calculate time duration between two times?
          Enter the start time and end time. The calculator subtracts the start from the end and returns the elapsed duration in hours, minutes and seconds. If the end time is earlier than the start, the overnight option adds 24 hours.
          
      
          Can this calculator handle overnight shifts?
          Yes. If the end time is earlier than the start time on the same date, the calculator treats it as an overnight shift and adds 24 hours.
          
      
          How do I calculate work hours with breaks?
          Use the Work Hours calculator. Enter clock-in and clock-out times, then add any paid or unpaid breaks.
          
      
          How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?
          Divide the minutes by 60. For example, 30 minutes is 0.5 hours, 15 minutes is 0.25 hours, and 45 minutes is 0.75 hours.
          
      
          Can I calculate overtime pay?
          Yes. The Overtime Calculator splits hours into regular, overtime and double time using rules you define. Jurisdiction templates are available as starting points.
          
      
          How are time zones handled?
          The Time Zone Converter uses the browser's built-in Intl APIs with IANA time zone identifiers. Daylight saving transitions are handled automatically.
          
      
          What is the Meeting Cost calculator?
          It estimates the real cost of a meeting based on the number of attendees, their average hourly rate, and the meeting length.
          
      
          Is my data stored online?
          No. Every calculation runs in your browser. History, saved calculations and favourites are kept in browser storage on your device.
          
      
          Does the calculator account for daylight saving time?
          Yes. The time zone converter is daylight-saving aware and flags transitions in destination zones.
          
      
          Can I embed a calculator on my own website?
          Yes. Click the Embed button on any result to get a copy-ready iframe snippet.
          
      
          Is this calculator a substitute for payroll advice?
          No. Overtime rules, tax rates and rounding conventions vary widely by country and employer. Verify results against your own local rules.
